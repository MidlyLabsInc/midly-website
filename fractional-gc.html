How Midly fits a fractional practice

Review faster. Keep the record. Bill from it.

Upload the counterparty’s contract and pick the side your client is on. Midly reviews it against your playbook and shows the terms that carry risk, each anchored to the exact clause so you can check it. The time you spend is captured against that client and document as you work, so the month you bill is the month you actually did. You make every call.

Counterparty paper, automated

The hardest review, made easy.

Drop the redlined .docx the counterparty sent. Midly extracts every insertion and deletion as a tracked change. Each one gets a risk level, a recommendation, and suggested counter-language ready to paste into your reply.

What you get on every redline
Risk-graded with grounded citations.

Every change the other side made is parsed out individually, classified low/medium/high risk, and tied back to the exact clause snippet it came from. No hallucinated case law. No invented contract history. Just the redline, the risk, the recommendation.

  • Accept, push back, or counter: one click per change
  • Suggested revision text generated against your playbook
  • Cited-snippet verification rejects insights that drift off-anchor
