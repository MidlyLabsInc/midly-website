Review links

This review link was sent with Midly.

Midly is one connected workspace for commercial contracts, from the first request to the next renewal. The team that sent you the link drafts, reviews, negotiates, signs and stores its contracts there. Here is what you just used, and what happens on their side.

See how teams use it Start a 7-day trial

The link you just used

A review link: the contract, without the account.

The other side sent this contract through Midly as a link rather than as another attachment. You did not need an account or a password to read it and answer.

  1. Opened in your browser.

    You opened the contract from the link and read it there, with nothing to install and no sign-up.

  2. Comments and markup, on the document.

    What you had to say went onto the contract itself, not into a separate email or a renamed copy of the file.

  3. Approve, or request changes.

    When you finished, you approved it or asked for changes. Your response landed back on the same document the sender is working in, so there is no version to reconcile and nothing to dig out of email.

On the sending side

One workspace, from the first draft to the signed agreement.

The review link is one step. Each stage hands the next one everything it learned, so nothing is re-explained, re-found, or re-argued from nothing.

  1. 01

    Draft

    Generate from templates, send for counterparty review, get approved, and execute, all from one deal room.

  2. 02

    Review

    AI Insights grades every clause against the team’s own playbook and anchors each finding to the exact language, read from the side they are on.

  3. 03

    Negotiate

    Word markup arrives as real tracked changes, accepted or rejected where they sit. Edits go back as tracked changes in the editor’s name, with the argument beside the clause.

  4. 04

    Sign

    eSignature is built in. Signers go in the order the sender sets, each on their own link, and signed contracts archive themselves.

  5. 05

    Store

    The executed agreement is kept with its key terms pulled out: dates, governing law, payment terms, and the liability cap. Midly emails reminders before expiry.

Midly does the reading. The people using it keep the judgment. Every finding is tied to the clause it quotes, and they decide what goes into the document.
