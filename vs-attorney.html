The Template + ChatGPT Trap

A template from Google and a prompt to ChatGPT isn't review. It's a guess with better formatting.

Most businesses without a lawyer find contracts the same way: a template from a business school folder or a quick search, dropped into ChatGPT or Claude with "make this sound professional." It looks like a contract. It hasn't been checked against anything, not your risk tolerance, not the deal you actually negotiated, not what a specific clause has cost someone else before.

Midly isn't a prompt. It's an attorney-built playbook, refined over two years specifically on commercial contracts, applied the same way to every document you send.
