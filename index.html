The problem

Contracts don’t stall in the drafting. They stall in the gaps.

Most of the delay around a commercial contract has nothing to do with the clauses. It is the work of finding out what happened, who decided it, and which draft is real.

  1. Legal hears about it last.

    The deal has a close date before anyone in legal has seen the paper.

  2. The context never arrives.

    A draft lands with no note on what was promised on the call, or which side you are on.

  3. Nobody is sure which version is current.

    Three drafts in three inboxes, and a markup made against the wrong one.

  4. The decisions are buried in email.

    Why the cap moved lives in a thread half the people involved were never copied on.

  5. Signed, filed, forgotten.

    An executed agreement comes back out of the drawer once something has already gone wrong.

How Midly works

One connected contract lifecycle.

Each stage hands the next one everything it learned, so nothing is re-explained, re-found, or re-argued from nothing.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Forward contracts to your Midly intake inbox, or upload them, then file each one to the right client or matter. Tracked changes in Word files arrive intact.

  2. 02

    Review

    Every clause graded against your own playbook and anchored to the exact language, read from the side you are on.

  3. 03

    Negotiate

    Your edits go back as tracked changes in your name, with the argument beside the clause, and the discussion stays in one place.

  4. 04

    Store

    The executed agreement is kept with its key terms pulled out: dates, governing law, payment terms, and the liability cap.

  5. 05

    Monitor

    Expiry, auto-renewal and notice terms are pulled out, and Midly emails reminders before expiry and on the last day to give notice.

On the same agreement, the positions you took last round come with you into the next review. Findings are marked new, carried forward, or resolved, so a round does not start from nothing.

Inside Midly

Know which changes deserve your attention.

The markup comes back with a hundred changes in it and two of them move the risk. Midly rates every change by risk and calls it accept, push back or counter, so the few that matter stand out. You decide what goes into the document.

Beyond the signature

The agreement is signed. The intelligence shouldn’t stop.

Negotiated for weeks, then filed and never read again. Midly reads an agreement once it is marked executed, so your repository holds the terms you are actually living under, not a pile of files somebody would have to open.

  • What it says, without reading it again

    What kind of agreement it is, when it starts and ends, the governing law, the payment terms, the liability cap, and whether indemnities are uncapped.

  • The deadline comes and finds you

    Auto-renewal and the notice period are pulled out of clause 14, and Midly emails reminders before expiry and on the last day to give notice.

  • A version your client can read

    Email your client a plain-English summary of the key terms. Your playbook analysis stays with you.
